OUR MISSION

Building pathways toward healing, dignity and hope.

REWAVINA provides comprehensive resettlement services and support to women affected by violence across Africa, helping them rebuild their lives, restore their dignity, and become active contributors to their communities, while advocating for a violence-free continent.

Creating impact through action.
